# GTA Shorts Style Playbook (living document)

Distilled lessons from studied videos. **Read this before writing any script or edit.json.**
Each rule cites the video note(s) it came from (`knowledge/videos/*.md`). Update it after every study session:
add new rules, strengthen rules confirmed by several videos, and strike out rules that turn out wrong.

Confidence: ★ = seen once · ★★ = seen in 2–3 videos · ★★★ = consistent pattern across many videos

## Hook (0–3 s)
- ★★ First frame is already action or a recognisable GTA VI image; no intro, no logo-only card. [ref2, ref3]
- ★ The first spoken word starts at 0.0 s. [ref2]
- ★ Hook formula: "Here's N [adjective] things you can do in GTA 6 that you can never do in GTA 5." Number + contrast. [ref2]
- ★ Official game logo shown in the hook, circled in green. [ref2]

## Pacing
- ★ Narrated listicle: ~35 cuts/min, median shot 1.75 s, 40% of shots under 1.5 s. [ref2]
- ★ Narration is fast: ~240 wpm, no pauses between facts. [ref2]
- ★ Non-narrated comparison loops: 11–13 s total, 2–3 s per side, built to loop. [ref1, ref3]

## Captions
- ★ Heavy italic sans (Montserrat ExtraBold Italic is a close match), white, thin dark outline + soft shadow. [ref2]
- ★ Narrow column, 2–4 short lines, centre / lower-middle, words revealed as spoken. [ref2]
- ★ Green = the "wow"/positive word, red = danger/negative word; only 1–2 coloured words per caption. [ref2]

## Fact headings
- ★ Top centre: green "1." + white italic title (2 lines max) + one small emoji-style icon underneath. [ref2]
- ★ Captions disappear while the heading is up (~2 s). [ref2]

## Graphics
- ★ Game-HUD style elements (wanted stars panel, button-prompt list) reinforce the claim. [ref2]
- ★ GTA V shots are shown briefly as the "can't do" contrast and are clearly different-looking. [ref2, ref3]

## Ending
- ★ Ends on a question: "So which one are you gonna try first?" [ref2]
- ★ Last line rolls into a teaser ("And in case you missed it…") to feed the loop / next video. [ref2]

## Comparison / loop formats
- ★ 16:9 gameplay letterboxed in 9:16 with the game logo above each clip; V and VI alternate with matched actions (same car / jump / kayak). [ref3]
- ★ Meme format: white caption box at the top in black regular sans ("Modded GTA 5 spoiled me… now GTA 6's graphics ain't impressing me"); trending song; no narration. [ref1]

## What NOT to copy (our standards)
- ref2 presents leaks / rumours / possibly AI-generated clips as confirmed features. We label every claim
  (confirmed / reported / rumour) and never generate fake gameplay.
- Never reuse other creators' footage. Their videos are style references only.
