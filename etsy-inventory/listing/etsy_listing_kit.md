Q: I'm not good with spreadsheets. Is this hard?
A: Cream cells are for typing, green cells have dropdowns, and everything else calculates itself and is locked. Start Here walks you through setup in 5 steps.

Q: Are the Etsy fees up to date?
A: Fees are in an editable table on Start Here, preset to the standard Etsy US rates. If Etsy changes them, update one cell and every order recalculates.

Q: I don't sell on Etsy. Will it work for me?
A: Yes. Channels, fees, currency and tax region are all yours to set: in-store/POS, invoices, website, Shopify, markets, wholesale. Etsy is just one preset.

Q: Do I have to type every order?
A: No. Export your orders (Etsy, Shopify, or any system that gives a CSV), paste them into Order Import, and copy the READY rows into Sales. Duplicates are flagged automatically.

Q: I make to order. Does it still work?
A: Yes. Set "Deduct Materials When" to Sold, and materials are deducted when an order is logged instead of when you make a batch.

Q: Does the currency change work in Google Sheets?
A: Headers, KPI cards and charts show your currency symbol. In Google Sheets, table cells show plain numbers without the symbol (a Google Sheets limitation); all math is identical.

Q: Do I have to delete the sample data?
A: No. Just open the BLANK file and start typing. (Prefer to keep your sample-file settings? Start Here also lists a 60-second clear-out per tab.)

Q: Can I edit the formulas or add columns?
A: Yes. Sheets are protected without a password: Review > Unprotect Sheet.

Q: Is this tax advice?
A: No. The category mapping is a bookkeeping aid. Please confirm with your accountant or tax adviser.

Q: Can I get a refund?
A: Because this is a digital download, refunds aren't available, but message me with any question and I'll help you get set up.
