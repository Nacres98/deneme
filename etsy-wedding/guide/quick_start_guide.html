Open it

Microsoft Excel

  1. Download the files from Purchases & Reviews in your Etsy account.
  2. Double-click the BLANK file. If Excel shows Enable Editing, click it.
  3. Save a copy with your names, e.g. Our Wedding Planner.xlsx.

Excel 2016 or newer, Microsoft 365, Excel for Mac.

Google Sheets (free)

  1. Go to drive.google.com → New → File upload and pick the BLANK file.
  2. Open it, then choose File → Save as Google Sheets.
  3. Use the new Google Sheets copy. Share it with your partner or planner.

Works on phone and tablet in the Google Sheets app too.

Two colours to know

Cream cells: type here. Many have a dropdown arrow.

White cells: calculate automatically. Please don't type over them.

Your first five minutes

  1. Start Here: both first names, wedding date, venue, total budget, target guest count, currency.
  2. Start Here → contributors (optional): who is paying and how much they pledged (e.g. you two, parents).
  3. Budget Builder: check the suggested split. Change any % to fit your priorities (keep the total at 100%).
  4. Dashboard: your countdown and budget are already live. Come back here every week.

Dashboard
  1. Budget: add each cost: category, a unique item name (e.g. "Photographer"), vendor, estimate. When you sign, add the contract price.
  2. Payment Schedule: pick that item from the dropdown and add each payment: type, due date, amount, who pays.
  3. When you pay, set Paid? = Yes and the date. Budget, Who's Paying, Budget Builder and the Dashboard update.
StatusMeaning
OVERDUEDue date has passed and it's not marked paid.

NOT SCHEDULED (Budget)A cost with no payments planned yet. Add them on Payment Schedule.

Guests: Guest List → Seating Chart

  • One row per invitation. Set Plus-one? = Yes and the party size becomes 2.
  • Track save the dates, invitations, RSVP, meals (also for the plus-one), allergies, gifts and thank-you cards.
  • Seating Chart: name your tables and set the seats. Then pick a Table for each guest on the Guest List; names appear on the Seating Chart automatically. "+1" means two seats. Declined guests are left out.
  • Status shows OPEN, FULL or OVER CAPACITY.

Checklist, Vendors, Tips & the big day

  • Checklist: 55 starter tasks. Each has a When (e.g. "6-8 months") and gets a due date from your wedding date. Set Done? = Yes. Add your own tasks at the bottom.
  • Vendors: contacts, quotes and status. Quote rank shows "1 of 3" for the cheapest quote in a category.
  • Tips & Envelopes: choose % of bill, flat amount or per person. If gratuity is already in the contract, set it to Yes and the tip becomes 0. Ranges shown are common US suggestions, not rules.
  • Day-Of Timeline: a starter schedule. Change times and names, then print or share it.

FAQ & tips

Good to know

A payment says "(not in Budget)". The item name on Payment Schedule doesn't match any Budget item. Pick it from the dropdown, or fix the spelling on Budget. Item names should be unique (duplicates turn red).
How many rows are there? Budget 200 items · Payments 300 · Vendors 100 · Guests 400 invitations · Tables 40 (12 invitations listed per table) · Checklist 150 · Tips 30 · Timeline 60.
Can I sort or filter? Yes, every list has filter arrows in its header row. Sorting the Guest List is fine; the Seating Chart follows.
Can I rename categories? Yes, on Budget Builder. The Budget and Vendors dropdowns use the new names. Re-pick the category on rows you already entered.
Can I change the currency? Start Here → Currency. Headers and Dashboard show the new symbol. Amounts are not converted.
Why is the Dashboard locked? It's protected (no password) so formulas don't get overwritten by accident. Excel: Review → Unprotect Sheet. Google Sheets may show a warning when you edit it.
The "Report date" on Start Here should stay blank: the planner then uses today's date. The Sample file has a fixed date so the example always looks the same.
Dropdown says "Pick from list". That column only accepts list values. Vendor and Meal columns allow your own text.
